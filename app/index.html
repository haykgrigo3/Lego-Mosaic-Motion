Video To Mosaic

LEGO Mosaic Motion Studio

Build a clean, larger-format Lego mosaic motion video from a short source clip. Load a video, frame the first shot, tune the palette and contrast, then render a silent mosaic video plus optional PNG frames.

Input

Load a short MP4 or WebM clip. The first frame becomes the live preview for framing and color tuning.

  • Start with 6 to 10 FPS for motion clips.
  • Tighter crops and higher contrast usually read better in mosaic form.
  • Renders are capped to 300 frames for this MVP to keep memory reasonable.

Framing And Output

Set the mosaic size, aspect, crop zoom, and output cadence. The crop overlay updates on the first frame.

Palette And Look

Use a consistent Lego palette across the whole clip. Dithering is optional, but off is usually steadier.
